# H02 exterior form study — H01 rejected

Status update: Daniel subsequently selected the offset tower set forward over the southeast corner. H02's northeast position is superseded by H03; this model remains an intermediate reference.

Daniel's feedback on H01 was: **"it is not good."** No more specific reason has yet been supplied. The diagnosis below is the assistant's assessment, not an attribution of additional preferences to Daniel. H01 is withdrawn as the recommended scheme and retained as development history.

## What was wrong with H01

The large tower/deck assembly dominated the main house, concealed the shed-roof form and weakened the greenhouse's place in the composition. The heater and stair were geometric placeholders with little of the intended Hearthtree expression. Increasing the early 672 sq ft main footprint to 1,122 sq ft was not justified by a convincing architectural result. Passing sampled geometry checks did not establish that it was a good design.

## What this study changes

- Sets the tower toward the northeast and the front of its deck 7 ft behind the main south wall. The main shed is visible south and west of it.
- Tests a 30 x 32 ft main enclosure, 16 x 14 ft tower and full nominal 6 ft deck ring. These are study variables, not approved requirements. Main enclosure: 960 sq ft; tower enclosure: 224 sq ft; deck ring: 504 sq ft.
- Restores a 16 ft south-high / 10 ft north-low main roof and taller south glazing above the greenhouse. The metal roof form, timber frame and repeated greenhouse sash are legible in the study.
- Relates the sheltered east work edge to the outboard deck supports.

This is **only an exterior form study**. It is not a replacement coordinated design package. The main-floor partitions, bedroom program, upper floor, stair, hearth sculpture, chimney penetrations and final load paths are unresolved. The model deliberately does not include a fabricated upper floor or staircase presented as a solved access route. Doors and windows are openings in the exterior study geometry, but no full envelope or weatherproofing claim is made. Materials indicate color and component relationships; they are not a finished material specification. Ground is illustrative, with no selected property.

The next coordinated plan must reconsider the three-bedroom evidence in revision 681. H01's two-bedroom recommendation was never approved. A smaller tower needs a genuinely workable stair and useful remaining room area; those requirements cannot be solved by shrinking the old geometry indiscriminately. Keep the full ring usable. No spiral, winder or conventional stair has been newly selected by Daniel.
