## The vertical conflict matters

The June 29 sheet describes **two stacked upper spaces**: a loft over the kitchen and a tower room above that loft. In contrast, v10.11 records an intermediate landing at **+8 ft 8 in** and tower floor at **+12 ft**. Those elevations are only 3 ft 4 in apart; they cannot serve as two full occupied rooms on the same footprint. That study uses a landing/bridge arrangement.

H03's **+19 ft** tower/deck level has not been justified by a resolved stair and room section. The next section must reconcile the intended upper spaces, south roof, greenhouse, stair headroom and full ring. Do not silently combine incompatible versions or delete the loft to make the exterior fit.
