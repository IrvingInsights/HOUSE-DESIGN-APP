# UX Deep-Review — 2026-07-31

Reviewed against `UX_REVIEW_BRIEF.md`, live, against the running "reimagined"
build (`http://127.0.0.1:5184/reimagine.html` → `src/reimagine/App.jsx`).
`classic.html` was explicitly out of scope (Daniel's call — stays untouched
until this app's core design/functionality is solid.

**Method:** driven with a real headless Chromium (Playwright) against the
live server, screenshotted at each step, with DOM/computed-CSS inspection to
confirm suspected bugs rather than guess from appearance alone. Not a code
read — findings were observed, then (where useful) traced back to source to
explain *why*.

Two sandbox caveats, not app bugs: Google Fonts fail to load with no internet
(fallback fonts render fine); a few nav clicks needed a direct JS click to
get past Playwright's strict actionability checks, likely due to WebGL/
software-rendering load (console showed repeated "GPU stall due to
ReadPixels" warnings) — not counted as a finding, but possibly worth a look
separately.

Review-only, per the brief ("do not implement changes") — nothing in this
pass touched app code. Fixes are a separate, later, explicitly-scoped pass.

**Corrections after an automated Codex review of this document (2026-07-31):**
this pass did NOT complete two of the brief's required Journey C steps —
sizing/positioning the upper floor and placing a loft + tower over one bay
(both correctly marked "Untested" below, not "pass") — so Journey C's
plan-first, multi-storey workflow does not have a full verdict yet; treat
that as a real gap in this review, not just in the app. Also: an earlier
draft of finding #6 and the friction log below scored the *absence of the
opening/greeting card* as part of the failure — that's wrong. `AGENTS.md`
records a standing rule: "Do not re-add a greeting card. He killed it once
on sight." The greeting card being gone is a deliberate decision, not a bug;
only `TESTING.md` still describing one is the actual defect (folded into
finding #1). Finding #6 below is corrected to be about the separate, still-
real issue: no way to reach a truly blank/empty-land design, independent of
any greeting screen.

## 1. Top-10 findings

**#1 — The onboarding doc describes an app that doesn't exist in this build**
*Journey: F (touches B, E). Severity: critical.*
`TESTING.md` — what a second user is told to read — instructs "the opening
card → Start on empty land," "open the chat and say 'add two bedrooms...'," 
"Start from a drawing" (PDF trace), "Review tab → one-tap fix," "Layers →
Exploded view," "Export → Permit set / Frame drawings / IFC." None of these
exist in this build: no opening card, no chat panel (zero matches for "chat"
in `App.jsx`), no PDF upload/trace, no "Review" tab, no Layers/Slice/X-ray/
Explode, no Export/Permit/IFC/Blender anywhere (zero matches for those terms
in source). A friend following this doc hits "wait, where is—" in the first
two minutes, on the document meant to build their trust.
**Fix:** rewrite `TESTING.md` against what this build actually does, or
re-wire chat/PDF/Export back in if they're meant to still exist.

**#2 — The 3D camera defaults to a broken, disorienting close-up**
*Journey: A, D, E. Severity: critical.*
Clicking "3D" from the very first page (Shape) lands on an extreme close-up
of the underside of the roof — no walls, no ground, no house, just texture.
Reproduced fresh, after a wait, and after re-toggling. Recurs on Roof,
Systems, and Finishes pages, and inside "Watch it build" (Week 1 shows an
equally extreme close-up of blank dirt). "Corner" recovers a sane framing,
but nothing tells the user that, and nothing does it automatically.
**Fix:** auto-fit the camera to the whole shell whenever 3D is entered or a
system page changes, instead of preserving prior zoom state.

**#3 — The "Worth a look" flags popup is completely invisible when opened**
*Journey: A, E (scar tissue: silent failure). Severity: critical.*
The only path to see/fix flagged issues (Journey E). Clicking it produces
nothing visible. Confirmed via computed styles: the popup *does* render with
real content ("Framed wall has no frame to carry it," with a fix button),
but its parent `.st-rail` has `overflow: hidden` at 128px wide while the
popup is 340px wide starting past the container's edge — 100% clipped, every
time. This is the exact "silent failure" scar tissue named in the brief.
**Fix:** `overflow: visible` on an ancestor, or render in a portal outside
the clipped rail — small CSS fix, currently a fully broken feature.

**#4 — Dragging a room in the Plan can pile it on top of other rooms with
zero warning**
*Journey: C (scar tissue: "things not staying put"). Severity: critical.*
Adversarially dragging the Kitchen room: it can end up fully overlapping the
Primary Bedroom, Bath Core, and Great Room simultaneously — rendered as
overlapping translucent rectangles, no collision guard, no "this doesn't
fit" message (only a door/wall-adjacency message, unrelated to the overlap).
On a smaller drag: the numeric FROM WEST/FROM NORTH fields updated to a new
value while the drawn position didn't move — numbers and picture disagreed.
This breaks the plan-first workflow (Law 8) under normal use.
**Fix (corrected):** do NOT auto-relocate a dropped room — `src/placement.js`
already carries a "LAW OF PLACEMENT": a drop is honored at its coordinates,
or the house grows to honor it, or a shaped outline lawfully trims it;
"anything else is a bug." Silently clamping/snapping a room away from where
the user dropped it would reintroduce the exact "things not staying put"
failure this brief calls out as scar tissue. Instead: honor the drop, and
make the resulting overlap **visible** (a warning outline on the overlapping
rooms and/or a Review flag), the same "advice, not silent failure" pattern
the Budget modal already uses. Separately, still fix the numeric-field/
drawn-position desync — that's a genuine two-sources-of-truth bug,
unaffected by this correction.

**#5 — Two live editors for the same Width/Depth number, open at once**
*Journey: A (Law 3 — "confirmed past complaint, treat any recurrence as
severe"). Severity: critical.*
Opening the "designs" menu on Shape overlays a panel with its own WIDTH/
DEPTH fields directly on top of the still-editable top toolbar's Width/Depth
fields — both live, both showing the same number, at once.
**Fix:** the designs/history flyout shouldn't overlap the live toolbar, or
should disable the toolbar fields while open.

**#6 — There is no way to reach a truly blank/empty-land design**
*Journey: A. Severity: high.*
Not about the (deliberately absent — see `AGENTS.md`'s "do not re-add a
greeting card" rule) opening screen. Even a brand-new session drops straight
into a fully-furnished five-room, $110,183 starter home. "+ New" (confirmed)
reloads the *exact same seed design*, not empty land — there is no UI path
to a truly blank plan at all, which matters because Law 8 names "the owner
arranges rooms in the 2D plan first" as the real workflow, and that workflow
currently has no empty starting point to arrange rooms *into*.
**Fix:** add a real "start on empty land" option distinct from the seed
template, or rename "+ New" to be honest about what it does.

**#7 — Escape doesn't close the cost/Budget modal**
*Journey: E. Severity: medium.*
The "Where the money goes" modal (otherwise genuinely good — see delight #1)
only closes via its small ×; Escape does nothing, and in testing it silently
blocked subsequent clicks until the × was found — reads as "the app stopped
responding."
**Fix:** wire Escape + background click to close, like other modals.

**#8 — The left rail doesn't follow the prescribed identity → verdict →
mode → view → system-map order**
*Journey: ambient, all. Severity: medium-high (Law 7).*
Today: [undo/redo, designs, "Classic look," version stamp] → [all 10
system-map chapters] → [flags + running total, at the very bottom]. The
verdict tiles the brief wants seen early sit below the entire chapter list.
No Design/Build toggle in the rail (Build is a floating bottom-right button)
and no Plan/3D/Storeys tabs in the rail — they live in a separate bottom
strip.
**Fix:** move flags/total to sit right under identity, above the chapter
list.

**#9 — Journey D's promised interaction set (Layers presets, Slice, X-ray,
Explode) doesn't exist**
*Journey: D. Severity: medium.*
What exists: a single "Show:" dropdown with five fixed states (Finished /
With the frame / Frame & foundation / Just the frame / No roof) — a
reasonable simpler substitute, but not what the brief/TESTING.md describe;
no slice slider, no x-ray, no explode anywhere.
**Fix:** either scope Journey D's expectations down to match what's shipped,
or treat as a real backlog item if these were meant to survive the reimagine.

**#10 — Two overlapping, confusingly similar "history" lists**
*Journey: A/C (Law 2). Severity: medium.*
The designs flyout shows "My designs" (explicit saves) directly above "EVERY
SAVE, KEPT ON THIS COMPUTER" (autosave), both using names like "Untitled
Natural Building Study — saved…". Confirmed autosave entries appear just
from opening the app and leaving it idle — no user action. Two lists, same
apparent job.
**Fix:** merge into one clearly-labeled list, or hide the autosave list by
default.

## 3. Law-by-law verdict

| Law | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Don't break anything > everything works > beauty | **Fail** | Invisible flags popup, room-overlap drag, broken default camera are function-breaking, not cosmetic. |
| 2. Everything it needs, nothing it doesn't | **Partial** | Content is lean (10 clear system pages), but two overlapping history lists (#10) add real redundancy. |
| 3. One editor per thing | **Fail** | Confirmed recurrence of "two open surfaces editing the same parameter" — Width/Depth editable in two places at once (#5). |
| 4. Plain numbers first | **Pass** | Every system page visited leads with obvious dimensions/presets before specialist detail. |
| 5. Everything grabbable, nothing mysterious | **Partial** | Tap/drag work once visible, but the invisible popup and camera issues make plenty currently mysterious. |
| 6. Numeric variables must always be workable | **Fail** | The room-drag bug produces a numeric field the drawing doesn't honor. |
| 7. Left bar reads in intelligent sequence | **Fail** | Verdict tiles sit below all 10 chapters, not near the top; no mode/view tabs in the rail. |
| 8. Plan-first is the real workflow | **Fail (for now)** | The one plan-first action tested — dragging a room — is the most broken interaction found. |
| 9. The app must be able to do it | **Fail** | Chat, PDF trace, and export/IFC are reachable **zero** ways, not gaps in one channel. |

## 4. Three delight opportunities

1. Extend the Budget modal's warm, first-person voice ("Each one takes the
   labour out of that line and puts it on your own back") to "Worth a
   look" once it's visible — the copy ingredient is already there
   ("Advice, not stop signs").
2. A brief, fading confirmation note in the "So far $X" ticker style
   whenever something big happens (a floor added, a wall system changed) —
   cheap, reinforces every click "visibly worked."
3. Once a true blank start exists, let "Watch it build"'s narration voice
   ("Week 1 of 17 — Site Prep & Excavation underway") carry into that empty
   moment — "Nothing built yet — where should the first wall go?" — instead
   of silently handing over someone else's floor plan.

## 5. Plain-language summary

The actual design screens — Shape, Storeys, Rooms, Foundation, Walls, Frame,
Roof, Systems, Finishes — are genuinely well built: plain numbers up front,
an honest cost breakdown with DIY checkboxes, a nice week-by-week build
timeline.

Four things are broken badly enough to stop a new user cold:

- **3D opens looking broken** — an extreme close-up of the roof underside,
  no house visible, every time. "Corner" fixes it, but nothing says so.
- **"Worth a look" shows nothing when clicked.** The popup is really there —
  it just draws itself off-screen. A real bug, not user error.
- **Dragging a room in the floor plan can drop it right on top of another
  room**, no warning, no stopping it — and arranging by dragging is supposed
  to be the main workflow.
- **Two boxes for the same number can be open at once** (house width/depth,
  shown twice when the designs menu is open) — the exact "two editors, one
  thing" complaint, back again.

Separately: `TESTING.md`, the file meant to hand a friend, describes a chat
box, a "trace a PDF floor plan" feature, an opening welcome screen, and
export-to-PDF/permit-drawings — **none of which exist in this build right
now.** The opening screen was deliberately removed on purpose (not up for
debate); the other three are worth a deliberate decision — bring them back,
or rewrite the doc/plan to match what's actually here today.

Nothing here is unfixable — most of it is small and specific once you know
where it is. But right now, a new user's first hour would likely include
several "wait, why can't I—" moments, on the exact list you care most about
avoiding.
